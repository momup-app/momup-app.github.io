# Who is going

The **See who is going** section on the home page lists upcoming events that at least one mom has said she's going to, with the chat names of everyone going.

- In an event's window, logged-in moms tap **I'm going**, confirm, and the event appears in the section with their name. **I'm not going anymore** takes it back.
- Only logged-in members see who is going, and only by their chat name (the **Name** on *My account*). People without a name show as "A mom". Nothing about the kids is shown.
- Visitors who aren't logged in see a "Log in" link instead.
- Past events drop off on their own. Each person can be going to at most 50 upcoming events.

## One-time setup (Supabase → SQL Editor)

Paste all of [`supabase/006_going.sql`](../supabase/006_going.sql) and click **Run**. Until then, the section says "“Who is going” is opening soon."
