Kids Nearby on your phone

Put it on your home screen: it opens full-screen like an app, works offline, and is free. No App Store needed.

✓ Kids Nearby is installed on this device.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add”. The MomUp icon appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap “Install” above, or the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Choose “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
  4. Confirm. The MomUp icon appears with your other apps.
